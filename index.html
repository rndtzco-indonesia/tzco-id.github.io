75+ Years of

Taiyuan Heavy Machinery Group Co., Ltd. (TZCO), founded in 1950, was China's first independently designed and built heavy machinery manufacturing enterprise.

Focusing on high-end, intelligent, and green solutions, TZCO has delivered over 3,000 product lines—exceeding 40,000 units and 10 million tons of equipment—for major national projects. The company has also pioneered over 500 first-of-their-kind products both in China and internationally.
